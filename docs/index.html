How accurate is this?

About 4 in 5 markers are real clearing (precision 0.80, 95% CI 0.53–0.97, measured at 10 m against Hansen Global Forest Change).

About 2 in 3 real clearings are missing. Recall is 0.32. An area with no marker has not been shown to be undisturbed — this map cannot be used as evidence that nothing happened.

Detections lag by roughly a year: the underlying annual imagery is published months after the year it covers. This is a record, not a real-time alarm.

What it is

India has no active near-real-time deforestation alert system. RADD does not cover India; the Forest Survey of India's Anavaran portal stopped updating in November 2025.
